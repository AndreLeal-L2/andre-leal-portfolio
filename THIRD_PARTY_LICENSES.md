# Third-party assets

## Retro computer geometry and baked lighting

- Source: [Retro Computer Personal Website](https://github.com/edhinrichsen/retro-computer-website)
- Author: Edward Hinrichsen
- Original files: `public/models/Commodore710_33.5.glb`, `public/textures/bake-quality-5.jpg`, `public/textures/bake_floor-quality-3.jpg`
- Local files: `public/assets/retro-computer.glb`, `public/assets/computer-bake.jpg`, `public/assets/computer-floor.jpg`
- Computer design based on the Commodore PET 8296.
- Camera movement, scroll choreography, CRT morphing, screen noise and phosphor shaders, bloom pipeline and drag controls are adapted from the original MIT-licensed implementation.
- The original environment cubemap is included in `public/assets/environmentMap/`.
- Screen content and the portfolio terminal bridge identify André Leal and are new. The portrait at `public/assets/andre-portrait.png` was supplied by André for this portfolio.
